Hair loss usually starts at the scalp rather than the strand, which is why this is formulated as skincare: centella to settle an irritated, flaking scalp and peptides to support the follicle underneath.

A clinical-solution tube with a nozzle that parts the hair, so the product reaches the scalp instead of sitting on the lengths.

  • Targets thinning and shedding at the root
  • Calms an itchy, flaking scalp
  • Peptides support healthier regrowth
  • Nozzle applies directly to the scalp, not the hair

  • Centella asiatica
  • Peptide complex
  • Niacinamide

The full ingredient list is printed on the carton. Ask us for a photograph of it before you order and we will send one.

  1. Part dry or towel-dried hair and apply along the scalp.
  2. Massage in with your fingertips for a minute.
  3. Leave in - there is no need to rinse.
  4. Use once or twice daily, consistently, for at least eight weeks.
