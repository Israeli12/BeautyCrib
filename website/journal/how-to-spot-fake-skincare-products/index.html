How to spot fake skincare products

The difference between a genuine product and a convincing copy is rarely the box. It is everything around it.

The Beauty Crib Editors 24 July 2026 7 minute read

Counterfeit cosmetics are a real problem across the region, and the imitations are no longer crude. Printing is sharp, holograms are copied, and photographs used in adverts are simply lifted from the brand. The reliable checks have moved elsewhere.

Check the batch code first

Genuine products carry a batch code printed or stamped on both the carton and the bottle, and the two should match. Free online batch-code checkers will decode manufacture dates for most major brands. A missing code, a code that appears on a sticker only, or a mismatch between box and bottle are all reasons to stop.

Read the small print, not the front

  • Ingredient lists on genuine packaging are printed crisply, in full, and in a consistent typeface.
  • Spelling and grammar errors in the small print are a strong signal, because brands proof this text carefully.
  • Check that the country of manufacture and the importer details are present and plausible.
  • Compare the shade of the packaging with the brand's own website. Counterfeits often drift a tone or two.

Trust the texture and the seal

Open it. A genuine product will have an intact inner seal, the pump or dropper will work smoothly, and the texture and scent will match what the brand describes. Copies are frequently thinner, more strongly perfumed to mask a cheap base, and separate in the bottle.

If the price looks like a miracle, it is usually a warning.

Price, and where you buy

Imported skincare carries freight, duty and handling before it reaches a shelf in Kampala. A price far below every other seller is not a bargain, it is information. The most reliable protection is the boring one: buy from a seller who will answer a question about sourcing, who accepts returns, and who can be found again next month.

That is the standard we hold ourselves to. Every product we list is sourced through channels we can account for, and if anything you receive from us looks wrong, we want to hear about it and will replace it.

In short

  • Match the batch code on the carton to the one on the bottle.
  • Read the small print for print quality, spelling and importer details.
  • Check seals, pumps, texture and scent as soon as you open it.
  • Be suspicious of prices far below the market, and buy from sellers you can return to.
