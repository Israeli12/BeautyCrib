04 — Philosophy

Confidence is quiet. So is good skin.

i

Authentic

Counterfeit cosmetics are common here. We would rather list fewer products than list ones we cannot vouch for.

ii

Curated

Seventeen cleansers, not seven hundred. Each one exists on the shelf because it answers a specific need.

iii

Considered

We explain what a product does, who it suits and when to skip it, including when the answer is do nothing.

iv

Self-care

A routine should take minutes, not evenings. The best one is the one you will still be doing in June.
