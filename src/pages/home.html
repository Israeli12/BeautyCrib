01 — The house

A shelf is easy to fill. Choosing well is the work.

We buy what we would use ourselves, we check where it came from, and we tell you honestly what it does. Nothing arrives here because it trended.

Beauty Crib is an online beauty shop based in Kampala, offering skincare, makeup, hair care, body care and fragrance to customers across Uganda. Every product is sourced genuine and sent out with the kind of advice you would get from someone who actually uses it. Start with a cleanser, build the rest slowly.
