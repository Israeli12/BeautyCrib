Delivery areas and timing

We deliver to every district in Uganda. Kampala, Wakiso and Entebbe are covered by our own riders, and everywhere else goes through a courier partner.

Delivery charges

Delivery is charged by area and confirmed with you before your order is dispatched, so you always know the total before anything is sent. Collection in Kampala is free.

If you would like the charge confirmed before placing an order, message us with your area and we will quote it.

How delivery works

  1. You place your order and receive an email confirmation with your order number.
  2. We confirm stock, payment and the delivery charge for your area.
  3. Your order is packed and dispatched, and we message you when it is on its way.
  4. Our rider or the courier calls the number on your order when they arrive.

Please use a phone number that will be reachable on the day, and add a landmark to your address. Landmarks are far more useful to a rider than a street name.

Collection

Choose pick up at checkout and we will message you when your order is ready, usually within 24 hours. Collection is by appointment so that someone is there to hand it to you.

Missed deliveries

If we cannot reach you, the rider will try again the same day and we will call to arrange a new time. After two failed attempts we hold the order for collection and contact you to agree what happens next.

Packaging

Orders are packed in plain, unbranded outer packaging. Glass bottles are wrapped individually. If anything arrives damaged, photograph it and contact us within 48 hours and we will replace it.

Delivery charges and timings are set by Beauty Crib and may change during public holidays or heavy rain. See also returns and refunds.
