Authenticity

Yes. We buy through suppliers we can account for and check batch codes on both carton and bottle. If anything you receive looks wrong, tell us and we will replace it.

Match the batch code on the box to the one on the bottle, check the print quality of the small text, and inspect seals and texture. Our guide on spotting fakes covers it in detail.

Many Korean products are sold in their domestic packaging. That is normal and is not a sign of a counterfeit. Ask us and we will send a photograph of the ingredient list and directions.

Returns

Unopened products in their original packaging can be returned within 7 days. For hygiene reasons opened skincare and cosmetics cannot be returned unless they are faulty or incorrect.

Contact us within 48 hours with your order number and photographs. We will arrange a replacement or refund at no cost to you.

Opened products cannot be returned, but message us. We will help you work out what caused it and how to adjust your routine, and we will factor that into what we recommend next.
